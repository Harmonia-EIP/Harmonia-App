Detailed Description

Synthesizer voice and sound definitions for Harmonia.

Included classes:

  • HarmoniaSound
  • HarmoniaVoice: a juce::SynthesiserVoice that plays a note on one of the two v3 engines

Both engines are plain C++ and match what the AI models are trained on (Harmonia-AI):

  • analog (engine/HarmoniaEngineV3.h, reference HARMONIA/src/synth/engine_v3.py): 2 oscillators x unison, noise, 12/24 dB filter, exponential envelopes, pitch envelope, LFO, drive, at twice the sample rate; chorus, delay and reverb run once on the mix, in the processor;
  • DX7 (engine/HarmoniaDx7.h): one DX7 voice played by Dexed's msfa FM core, as on the instrument.
